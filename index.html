Project overview

The world is a violent place plagued by conflict. This project helps readers grasp the different types of violence inflicted on civilians in recent years. Through interactive visualizations we unpack the tragic reality of modern conflicts, their human cost, and the patterns that emerge from systematic data collection.

About the data

We rely on the ACLED (Armed Conflict Location & Event Data Project) — a globally trusted source of political violence and protest records.

Key measures covered in this project include:

  • Fatalities by conflict type and region
  • Civilian vs. combatant casualties
  • Geographic distribution and temporal cadence of violence
